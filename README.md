## 🚀 Sovereign Timeline Blueprint (2006 – 2040)

### 🟢 Phase I: Genesis Node (2006 – 2017)
- **2006:** Born on April 30 in Mamuzai, Orakzai Agency, KPK, Pakistan.
- **2010:** Family relocation to Kohat due to regional conflict; early experience in rural asset management.
- **2011-2017:** Dual education in traditional systems (Madrassa) and modern sciences (Yahya Public School).

### 🔵 Phase II: Metropolitan Shift (2018 – 2023)
- **2018:** Executed a high-risk transition to Karachi at age 12; entered the real estate micro-markets.
- **2019-2021:** Established **Orakzai Group** umbrella; expanded real estate nexus across Karachi, Peshawar, and Kohat.
- **2022-2023:** Pivoted to global technology; enrolled at **Ziauddin University** for elite structural academic paradigms.

### 🟡 Phase III: Blockchain Artifacts (2024 – 2026)
- **2024-2025:** Evolved Orakzai Group into a tech infrastructure lab; expanded into UAE/Dubai networks.
- **Early 2026:** Launched **OrakzaiX** & **AdamX** (AI automation frameworks).
- **April 2026:** Deployed **Orakzai Bond (OKBOND)** natively on Polygon L2.
- **June 2026:** Acquired **Shamim Forever** luxury brand; integrated cryptographic provenance.
- **Current:** **Stevie Gold Award Winner 2026** (Best Young Entrepreneur).

### 🔴 Phase IV: Sovereign Projections (2027 – 2040)
- **2027-2030:** Full deployment of **OreC Protocol** for institutional real estate tokenization.
- **2031-2035:** Global launch of **OKBOND OTC** ecosystem application.
- **2036-2040:** Transitioning Orakzai Group into a fully autonomous, cross-border digital nation asset class.
